THESE ARE SHIPPED PRODUCTION ASSETS. Vite copies `frontend/public/` verbatim
into `dist/`, and the API serves `dist/` from its StaticFiles mount, so these
files are delivered to every visitor at `/fonts/…` and this notice at
`/fonts/OFL.txt`. `src/styles/fonts.css` and `public/about.css` carry the
matching `@font-face` rules. They were test fixtures until #365; serving them
from our own origin is what removes the render-blocking third-party stylesheet
that used to leave a visitor on a blank page while fonts.googleapis.com was
slow. `tests/fixtures.ts` still reads them from here, so there is exactly one
copy of the bytes and the e2e suite renders what production renders.
